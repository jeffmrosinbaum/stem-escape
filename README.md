# STEM Lab Escape Challenge

An outdoor STEM escape-room web app for 1st, 2nd and 4th grade groups, made for iPad Safari.
Play it here: https://jeffmrosinbaum.github.io/stem-escape/

- Kids open it from a QR code, tap their grade, and visit 10 playground stations. Each station unlocks a puzzle piece for the final "I Love STEM" puzzle.
- Kids' photos and answers stay only on the iPad until the finished record is emailed to the teacher (through a Google Apps Script). Nothing is uploaded to this repository.
- Teacher mode: press and hold the title for 2 seconds.
